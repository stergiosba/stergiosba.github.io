I am a 5th year Ph.D. candidate in the [Human Oriented Robotics and Control](https://horc-lab.github.io/) lab at the [University of Delaware](https://www.udel.edu/). 
I received my Diploma in Naval Architecture and Marine Engineering from the National Technical University of Athens (NTUA) in 2019 and my M.S. in Robotics from the University of Delaware in 2025. 

My research focuses on enhancing the physical intelligence of autonomous robots using **Probing Physical Interactions**.
This framework enables diverse robotic systems to understand hidden properties of their environment and use them in **Deep Reinforcement Learning for planning and control**.
During my Ph.D., I have implemented planning and control algorithms for a range of applications, ranging from an autonomous probing agent for
**swarm leader identification** to developing a whole-body controller for **quadruped robots to manipulate stacks of objects**.
